# Adoption status

Supported 0.3.16 is a research prototype. 0.3.17 is unreleased. The latest
experimental candidate passes correctness and recovery but fails performance
controls and regression guards; service qualification remains unstarted.

Historical DuckDB analytic wins coexist with PostgreSQL mixed/write-tail losses
and sustained-load failures. No primary-production recommendation or universal
database ranking is established. [Version summary](VERSION_SUMMARY.md) and
[short experiment records](BENCHMARK_HISTORY.md) retain these limits.
